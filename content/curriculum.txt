## Basics
# Alphabet
A a = short “a” — ar (with) {say: a. ar}
Ā ā = long “aa” — māja (house) {say: ā. māja}
B b = like English b — bērns (child) {say: b. bērns}
C c = “ts” as in cats — cepure (cap) {say: c. cepure}
Č č = “ch” as in church (Indonesian c) — četri (four) {say: č. četri}
D d = like English d — diena (day) {say: d. diena}
E e = short “e” as in bed — es (I) {say: e. es}
Ē ē = long “ee” as in bed, held longer — nedēļa (week) {say: ē. nedēļa}
F f = like English f — futbols (soccer) {say: f. futbols}
G g = hard g as in go — galva (head) {say: g. galva}
Ģ ģ = soft g, like “dy” — ģitāra (guitar) {say: ģ. ģitāra}
H h = like English h — hokejs (hockey) {say: h. hokejs}
I i = short “i” as in sit — istaba (room) {say: i. istaba}
Ī ī = long “ii” as in see — rīts (morning) {say: ī. rīts}
J j = “y” as in yes — jā (yes) {say: j. jā}
K k = like k, without a puff of air — kafija (coffee) {say: k. kafija}
Ķ ķ = soft k, like “ty” — ķirši (cherries) {say: ķ. ķirši}
L l = like English l — lūdzu (please) {say: l. lūdzu}
Ļ ļ = soft l, like “lli” in million — ļoti (very) {say: ļ. ļoti}
M m = like English m — māte (mother) {say: m. māte}
N n = like English n — nē (no) {say: n. nē}
Ņ ņ = “ny” as in Indonesian nyanyi — kaimiņš (neighbor) {say: ņ. kaimiņš}
O o = usually “uo” in Latvian words — logs (window) {say: o. logs}
P p = like p, without a puff of air — piens (milk) {say: p. piens}
R r = rolled r, like Indonesian r — rīt (tomorrow) {say: r. rīt}
S s = like English s — saule (sun) {say: s. saule}
Š š = “sh” as in shoe (Indonesian sy) — šodien (today) {say: š. šodien}
T t = like t, without a puff of air — tēja (tea) {say: t. tēja}
U u = short “u” as in put — upe (river) {say: u. upe}
Ū ū = long “uu” as in moon — ūdens (water) {say: ū. ūdens}
V v = like English v — vīrs (husband) {say: v. vīrs}
Z z = like English z — zivs (fish) {say: z. zivs}
Ž ž = like “s” in measure — žurka (rat) {say: ž. žurka}
ai = like “ai” in aisle — maize (bread) {say: maize}
au = like “ow” in how — auss (ear) {say: auss}
ei = like “ay” in day — ceļš {say: ceļš}
ie = “ia” glide — piens (milk) {say: piens}
dz = as in adds — dzert (to drink) {say: dzert}
dž = “j” as in jam — džinsi (jeans) {say: džinsi}
